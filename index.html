✂ clearcut

Cut pauses, “uh/aaa”, stretched sounds and breaths, check every cut, then encode at the smallest size that still looks right. Nothing is uploaded: it all runs in this tab.

What to remove

Silero VAD, the same model and defaults as clearcut (75 ms padding, 180 ms shortest pause cut).

“uh”, “aaa” and stretched sounds

clearcut’s detector: a held “aaa” has a flat spectrum and a flat pitch. Higher steadiness or pitch values find more (and risk real words).

Unvoiced noisy bursts next to a pause.

Size (shrinkvid)
